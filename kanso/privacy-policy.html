Kanso Privacy Policy

App: Kanso — Daily Minimalist Puzzles (com.kanso.dailypuzzles)
Developer: Assoum Studios
Effective date: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information Kanso collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Kanso is designed to work fully without an account: you can play every puzzle offline without signing in.

1. Information stored only on your device

When you play without signing in, your progress (XP, streaks, solved puzzles, achievements, display name you choose, and sound settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

2. Information collected if you sign in with Google (optional)

Cloud sync and the leaderboard require signing in with Google. When you sign in, we use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to process:

  • Account information from Google: your name, email address, Google account identifier and profile photo URL. This is used to create and secure your account.
  • Game progress: display name, XP, streaks, solved-puzzle history, achievements and the players you follow. This is used to sync your progress between devices.
  • Leaderboard: your display name, XP, streak and solve count appear on the public leaderboard.

Your email address is never shown to other players.

3. Technical data

Like most online services, Google Firebase may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, device and app version, and request logs to deliver the service, prevent abuse and keep accounts secure. Kanso does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics, does not use your data for advertising, and does not sell your data.

4. Puzzles, sprints and reminders

Every daily puzzle is generated on your device. Arena pace sprints run entirely on your device against computer-controlled pace rivals. If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled locally by your phone's notification system; no data is sent to us. Sound, haptics and other settings stay on your device.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is processed by Google LLC (Firebase) as our service provider, under Firebase's privacy and security terms. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. Access is restricted by security rules so only you can read your private profile. We keep account data until you delete your account.

7. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: open the You tab, go to Account & sync and tap Delete account & cloud data. This permanently deletes your account, your cloud save and your leaderboard entry.
  • By email: send a deletion request from the email address linked to your account to assoumstudios@gmail.com with the subject “Kanso account deletion”. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

8. Children

Kanso is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
